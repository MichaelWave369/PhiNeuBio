What is being measured?

The tanh comparator is a fixed-weight recurrent reservoir. Neither it nor the spiking reservoir trains its recurrent connections. Their logistic readouts learn.

Input bits stay unchanged more often than they flip. This provides temporal redundancy, so history may help recover noisy targets.

Profiling measures elapsed Python-process time and peak traced Python allocations. It does not measure energy, CPU instructions, or total resident memory.
