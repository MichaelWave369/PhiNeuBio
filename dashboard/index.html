What changed?

A fixed digital leaky integrate-and-fire reservoir converts the cue into spike and voltage features. A logistic readout chooses an action and updates its weights after a binary reward. The reservoir itself does not learn.

Research limits and data contract

Requires PhiNeuBio schema 2.0 with a trace of simulated trials. The dashboard does not control living neurons or contact cloud services.

ΦNeuBio is an open research prototype. Learning-like software behavior is not evidence of consciousness. No external assets or tracking scripts.
