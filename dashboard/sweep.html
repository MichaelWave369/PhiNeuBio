What the ledger counts

Dense products: scalar products consumed by recurrent and readout computations.

Spike additions: previous-spike conditional weight additions in the LIF implementation, including zero weights.

Nonzero active edges: the subset where the LIF weight is actually nonzero. A potential sparse optimization opportunity, not a measured speedup.

Each model trains separately at each cap, with the same paired train, validation, test and probe streams.
