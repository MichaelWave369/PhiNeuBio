How differences change with training data

Positive means the first model has higher mean test accuracy. Whiskers are exploratory 95% seed-paired percentile bootstrap intervals, not Holm-corrected intervals.

Scientific boundaries

The preregistered research question appears when you load a report.

All model-selection decisions use independent validation streams. Increasing training data reuses a common prefix, while validation and test data stay fixed per seed.

Exact sign tests measure how consistently one architecture wins across seeds, not the mean effect magnitude. Holm adjusts sign-test p-values over the entire declared family.

A small p-value is not proof of superiority, and a large p-value is not evidence of equivalence. Models still have unmatched computational budgets.
