Held-out accuracy comparison

Mean accuracy across seeds; all models receive identical train/test input sequences.

What makes this different?

Trained RNN: recurrence, input projection and output layer learn from whole sequences using backpropagation through time (BPTT).

Spiking LIF and tanh reservoirs: internal recurrent connections stay fixed; only the output readouts learn.

Explicit memory: the history window and delayed tap directly store older observed inputs. They may be simpler and better.

This is software simulation. No claims about biological neurons, consciousness or electrical energy.

Trainable RNN learning curve

Per-epoch mean sequence cross-entropy for one seed and the selected noise/delay. It is training loss, not test accuracy.
