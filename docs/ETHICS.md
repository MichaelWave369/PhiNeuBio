# Ethics, reproducibility, and claims

- The software is a digital spiking network. It does **not** culture, stimulate, or control living human or animal cells.
- Behavioral adaptation, predictive accuracy, and neuronal firing do not by themselves establish awareness or subjective experience.
- Do not frame any future results as treatment, diagnosis, or proof of consciousness.
- Future live-tissue work belongs in independently governed, authorized facilities following applicable biosafety, consent, sourcing, animal/human research, data security, and ethics oversight.
- Any future physical stimulation interface must require explicit review, narrow permissions, auditable controls, and fail-safe limits; none are provided here.
- Publish failure cases, metrics, configurations, baselines, and limitations alongside successful demonstrations.
- Avoid hype and avoid releasing unreviewed research-hardware control pathways.

The repository exists to facilitate open, careful scientific and engineering investigation.
